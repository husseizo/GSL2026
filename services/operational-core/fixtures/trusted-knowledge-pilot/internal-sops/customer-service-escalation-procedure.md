# Customer Service Escalation Procedure

## When to escalate

A customer complaint must be escalated to the garage manager if it involves a safety concern, a repeat repair on the same fault, or a disputed warranty decision.

## Communicating uncertainty honestly

When a diagnosis is not yet confirmed, the customer must be told the finding is provisional, not presented as certain. A recommended repair must never be described using the same language as a required, safety-critical repair.

## Documentation

Every escalation must be logged with the customer's original complaint, the actions taken, and the final resolution. An escalation without a recorded resolution must not be closed.

## Refusing unsupported requests

If a customer requests a repair or part substitution that conflicts with a documented safety requirement or official approval, the request must be declined and the reason recorded, not silently accommodated.
