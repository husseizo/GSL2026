# Workshop Safety Procedure

## Personal protective equipment

Every technician must wear safety glasses, gloves, and steel-toed boots before working under a raised vehicle. Hearing protection is required when operating pneumatic tools for more than 10 minutes continuously.

## Lifting and jacking

Only certified two-post or four-post lifts rated for the vehicle's real gross weight may be used. Never work under a vehicle supported only by a hydraulic jack — always lower the vehicle onto axle stands rated for at least 2 tonnes per stand before working underneath.

## Fluid handling

Used engine oil, brake fluid, and coolant must be drained into clearly labelled containers and disposed of through the approved waste-oil contractor, never poured into a drain. A spill kit must be within 5 metres of every service bay.

## Fire safety

A Class B fire extinguisher must be mounted within 10 metres of every service bay. Technicians must know the location of the nearest extinguisher before starting any fuel-system work.
