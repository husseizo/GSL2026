# Lubricant Selection Procedure

## Real approval versus recommendation

An official manufacturer approval and a general recommendation are not the same thing and must never be presented as equivalent. A lubricant with a genuine OEM approval number for the vehicle's engine may be used without further sign-off. A lubricant that is only "recommended" for a similar application requires senior technician sign-off before use.

## Selection order

1. Check the vehicle's real, documented OEM approval requirement (viscosity grade and approval number).
2. Search the Knowledge Platform for a lubricant item carrying that exact approval.
3. If no exact approval match exists, do not substitute a similar-viscosity product without a documented sign-off from a lubricants specialist.

## Viscosity grade handling

Viscosity grades (for example 5W-30, 5W-40, 75W-90) must always be recorded exactly as printed on the product — never abbreviated or reformatted, since a misread grade can cause real engine damage.

## Quantity

The fill quantity used must match the vehicle's documented capacity, not the container size. Record both the documented capacity and the actual quantity used on the job card.
