# Vehicle Inspection Procedure

## Scope

This procedure covers the standard multi-point inspection performed at every scheduled service.

## Inspection order

1. Confirm the vehicle identity (VIN and registration) matches the job card before starting.
2. Inspect brakes, suspension, steering, tyres, and fluid levels in that order.
3. Record every finding as PASS, WARNING, or FAIL — never leave an item unrecorded.

## Safety-critical findings

Any FAIL finding on brakes, steering, or suspension must be flagged with a safety warning and communicated to the customer before the vehicle leaves the workshop, regardless of whether the customer approved the related repair.

## Recommended versus required

A WARNING finding results in a recommended action. A FAIL finding on a safety-critical system results in a required action. The two must never be presented to the customer as equivalent.
