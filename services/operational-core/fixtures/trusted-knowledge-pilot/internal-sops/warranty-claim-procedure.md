# Warranty Claim Procedure

## Eligibility check

Before opening a warranty claim, confirm the vehicle is within the manufacturer's real documented warranty period and that the failed component is covered under the specific warranty terms on file — not assumed from general knowledge.

## Evidence required

A warranty claim requires: the original complaint as reported by the customer, the diagnostic findings, the failed part number, and photographic evidence of the failure where practical. A claim without a recorded diagnosis must not be submitted.

## Distinguishing warranty from goodwill

A genuine warranty claim is covered by documented manufacturer terms. A goodwill repair is not — it must be labelled as goodwill, not warranty, so the two are never confused in reporting.

## Escalation

Any claim involving a safety-related failure must be escalated to the garage manager before submission, regardless of warranty status.
