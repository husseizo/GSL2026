# Quality Control Review Procedure

## Sampling

A real sample of completed jobs is reviewed each week by the quality-control reviewer, weighted toward safety-critical repairs and repeat-repair cases.

## Review checklist

The reviewer confirms: the recorded diagnosis matches the recorded symptoms, the parts and lubricants used match what was authorized, the torque specifications applied were sourced correctly, and the job card is complete before closing the review.

## Outcomes

Each reviewed job is classified as PASS, MINOR_ISSUE, or MAJOR_ISSUE. A MAJOR_ISSUE classification requires the technician's supervisor to be notified before the next job is assigned to that technician.

## Escalation to knowledge governance

If a quality-control review finds that an approved Knowledge Platform item led to an incorrect repair, the item must be flagged for re-review by a knowledge steward before it is used again.
